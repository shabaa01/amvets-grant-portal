HIPAA Standard for the Portal
Data handling policy — what can and cannot go into this tool
⚠ THIS PORTAL IS NOT A CERTIFIED HIPAA-COMPLIANT PLATFORM It runs entirely in your browser's local storage. It is intended as an operational coordination shadow-system only. All Protected Health Information (PHI) must live in the official VA Data Collection Tool and secure clinical channels.
✓OK to Store in Portal
  • Participant IDs (redacted, e.g., P-0142)
  • Enrollment date and cohort
  • Status flags (Active, High-Risk Watch, Referred, etc.)
  • VA-affiliation status at intake (yes/no)
  • Aggregate screening scores + categories
  • Timepoint labels (Baseline, 90-day, etc.)
  • Staff role/initials who administered
  • Referral counts + 72-hour compliance status
  • Event/outreach attendance counts
✕NEVER Store in Portal
  • Real names, SSNs, DOBs, addresses
  • DoD/VA ID numbers
  • Full clinical assessment narratives with identifiers
  • Detailed session/therapy notes
  • Suicide plan/method specifics
  • Family member names or contact info
  • Photos or scans of documents with PII
  • Medication lists
  • Financial account info
◇Full HIPAA Data Flow (Recommended Architecture)
  1. Point of Contact — Staff meets Veteran; obtains verbal consent for AHSPP enrollment.
  2. Paper Intake — Full-detail intake completed on paper (or secure DoD/VA form). Signed consent + release attached.
  3. VA Data Collection Tool Entry — Program Director / Case Manager enters full record with real identifiers into the official VA system (secure, HIPAA-compliant channel).
  4. Portal Entry (this tool) — Only the redacted Participant ID (linkable back to the paper record only through a locked crosswalk maintained by the Project Director) is entered here for internal coordination.
  5. Physical Records — Paper intake, screening completions, and case notes filed in the locked HIPAA-secured Participant Files (Records Binder 03).
  6. Retention — All records retained 3 years post-final expenditure report per 38 CFR § 78.150 and 2 CFR Part 200 Subpart D.
↗If You Want True HIPAA-Compliant CRM

To upgrade to a fully HIPAA-compliant CRM (encrypted-at-rest, audit-logged, BAA-covered, access-controlled), we recommend evaluating one of these platforms after FY27 is stable:

  • Salesforce Health Cloud — Nonprofit pricing; used by many Veteran-serving orgs; BAA available
  • Athena / eClinicalWorks — Full EHR functionality; higher cost; for clinical programs
  • CaseWorthy — Purpose-built for HHS-funded case management (SSVF, HUD, VA)
  • Apricot by Bonterra — Nonprofit case-management platform with HIPAA BAA option
  • Airtable Enterprise + BAA — Lightweight; requires custom access controls

Selection of a HIPAA-compliant CRM would require a written prior approval request to VA-OSP if funded from grant dollars (it would be a new activity outside the approved budget). Alternatively, funded from general operating dollars.

➤Program Design

AHSPP is a community-based, non-clinical suicide prevention initiative serving 150 at-risk Veterans across Honolulu County over a one-year grant period. Designed as a nontraditional intervention under 38 CFR § 78.85, the program leverages AMVETS Hawaii's established Adaptive Fitness Program at the West Oahu Veterans Center as the engagement platform for delivering baseline mental health screening, case management, trained Veteran peer support, outreach, gatekeeper education, lethal means safety counseling, warm hand-off referral to VA Pacific Islands Health Care System, and stability supports under 38 CFR § 78.90.
